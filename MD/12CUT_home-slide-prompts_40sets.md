# 12CUT Home Slide Film Prompts — 40 Story Sets

## Purpose

- Target: 12CUT home screen film-reel slide images
- Volume: 40 story sets x 12 slide images = 480 images
- Tools: Higgsfield with Nano Banana 2 or GPT Image 2.0
- Output: 4:3 landscape still images
- Mood: warm, cute, beautiful, pretty, emotionally intimate, unmistakably 12CUT

## Global Style Prefix

Paste this before every image prompt.

```text
Warm, cute, beautiful, pretty, emotionally intimate memory photo for a 12CUT slide. Soft Korean lifestyle mood, gentle happiness, lovely and heartwarming atmosphere, Kodak Portra 400 film look, warm golden-hour light, pastel cream tone, soft highlights, natural candid emotion, clean composition, shallow depth of field, delicate film grain, nostalgic but modern, 4:3 landscape photo, no text, no logo, no watermark, no 12CUT product visible.
```

## Global Negative Prompt

Paste this as the negative prompt for every image.

```text
cold mood, scary, dark gritty, harsh contrast, vulgar, sexy, provocative, messy background, distorted face, distorted hands, extra fingers, bad anatomy, uncanny smile, celebrity likeness, real idol likeness, text, watermark, logo, oversaturated colors, low quality, plastic skin, AI artifacts, 12CUT product, slide viewer, toy camera, product shot, holding a viewer
```

## Product Visibility Rule

These images are the memory photos that go inside the 12CUT slide. They must not show the 12CUT physical product.

```text
Never include the 12CUT slide viewer, the 12CUT product, a toy camera-like viewer, or people holding the 12CUT device inside slide images. The slide image should be the memory itself, not a photo of someone using the product. Cameras, phones, printed photos, cakes, letters, gifts, lightsticks, and everyday memory objects are allowed only when they belong naturally to the scene.
```

## Wardrobe Variation Rule

Use this rule for every story set. The goal is to keep 12CUT's warm, cute, beautiful tone while avoiding repetitive same-outfit images.

```text
Do not repeat the exact same outfit across story sets. Keep a coherent wardrobe within one 12-image story set, but vary outerwear, tops, textures, accessories, and seasonal styling between sets. Use warm cream, ivory, oatmeal, beige, blush pink, butter yellow, soft denim, sage, cocoa brown, muted sky blue, and gentle pastel accents. Outfits must look natural, cute, pretty, warm, and emotionally intimate, never flashy, luxury, sexy, dark, or street-aggressive.
```

## Prompt Assembly Formula

Use this order for each image.

```text
[Global Style Prefix] + [Theme Character Prompt] + [One Wardrobe Capsule for that story set] + [One scene prompt from the 12-image sequence] + [Global Negative Prompt]
```

## Character Prompts by Theme

### Lover

```text
A sweet Korean couple in their mid-20s, soft natural styling, gentle smiles, affectionate but modest body language, cute and beautiful romantic chemistry, sincere everyday love, natural seasonal wardrobe variation, no exaggerated posing, no luxury mood.
```

### Friends

```text
Two or three close Korean friends in their early-to-mid 20s by default, with occasional wider group shots only when the scene specifically needs it. Natural casual styling, soft pastel accents, playful but warm friendship, candid laughter, comfortable intimacy, cute gestures, varied outfits by occasion and season, no forced influencer pose, real shared memories.
```

### Friends Cast Size Rule

```text
Do not make every friends-theme image a large group scene. Most friends slides should feel like private memories between two or three close friends. Use four or more friends only for final portraits, party scenes, graduation group moments, or scenes where a wider group is explicitly meaningful. The emotional priority is intimate friendship, not crowd size.
```

### Friends Face Diversity Rule

```text
Friends must not look like twins or the same AI face repeated. Give each friend clearly distinct but natural facial features: different face shapes, eye shapes, smiles, hairstyles, hair length, bangs or no bangs, subtle skin tone variation, height, accessories such as glasses or hair clips, and different styling details. Keep everyone warm, cute, beautiful, and 12CUT-like, but avoid identical faces, duplicated identity, same hairstyle on everyone, same makeup on everyone, or clone-like symmetry.
```

### Friends Real Human Anti-AI Rule

```text
Do not reuse the exact same friend cast description across every slide in one set. Within each 12-image friends set, vary the people scene by scene: sometimes two friends, sometimes three friends, and only occasional wider group shots when meaningful. Each scene should describe different realistic identities rather than repeating Friend A/B/C. Use natural human asymmetry, different facial proportions, subtle skin texture, varied smiles, different styling, different posture, and candid imperfect timing. Avoid the polished AI-beauty look: no porcelain skin, no identical heart-shaped faces, no same V-line jaw, no same large eyes, no same nose and lips, no over-smoothed makeup, no model-catalog symmetry, no influencer clone feeling. The image must feel like a real candid memory photo of actual different friends, not an AI-generated set of similar pretty faces.
```

### Travel

```text
Korean travelers in their 20s or early 30s, soft travel styling, varied travel outfits by destination and weather, gentle curiosity, warm discovery, beautiful scenic moments, candid documentary feeling, personal memory rather than tourist advertisement.
```

### Travel Character Continuity Rule

```text
For a solo-traveler travel story set, keep the same traveler identity across all 12 slides. Do not change face shape, hair length, core hairstyle, age impression, or signature styling from scene to scene. Vary camera angle, distance, outfit layers, weather accessories, and whether the face is visible, but the traveler must still read as the same real person. Unlike Friends sets, Travel solo sets need character continuity first and face diversity second. Avoid AI-model polish while preserving identity: natural skin texture, slight asymmetry, candid posture, and realistic travel fatigue are good; changing the person is not.
```

### Pets

```text
A Korean pet owner with a beloved dog or cat, warm home and outdoor lifestyle, gentle bonding, cute pet expression, cozy wardrobe variation for home, walk, picnic, rain, and seasonal memories, soft natural light, emotionally intimate companion memory, no exaggerated cartoon look.
```

### Wedding

```text
A Korean bride and groom, elegant but warm wedding mood, varied styling across proposal, fitting, picnic, ceremony, and honeymoon, romantic candid emotion, beautiful natural smiles, delicate flowers, warm sunlight, intimate documentary wedding memory, not overly luxurious.
```

### Idol Fan Memory

```text
A fictional K-pop fan memory scene, no real celebrity likeness, no recognizable idol face, cute fan items, lightstick, photocard, concert ticket, varied fan outfits by concert, cafe, room, fan meeting, birthday support, and after-concert moments, warm friendship and excitement, beautiful youthful fandom memory, safe fictional idol culture.
```
